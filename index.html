Why consider solar?

Electricity prices can be volatile. Solar photovoltaic (PV) systems let you produce a portion of your electricity, lowering the portion you buy from the grid. For many households, especially with consistent daytime usage, a properly sized system reduces monthly bills and hedges against rising tariffs.

  • Lower monthly bills: offset your daytime consumption with solar generation.
  • Predictable long-term savings: known install cost vs uncertain future prices.
  • Environmental benefits: less grid electricity means smaller carbon footprint.

How to use the Solar Savings Tool

The tool helps you compare actual bills before vs after solar, estimate what you would have paid without solar, and calculate payback. It accepts monthly kWh and ₱/kWh values and saves sessions locally (CSV). To start, open the Solar Power Savings Tracker and follow the prompts.

The tool is built to be privacy-friendly: calculations are performed in your browser and saved to a CSV file you control. No data is sent to any server unless you explicitly upload it.

Realistic expectations

Payback depends on system cost, financing terms, and how much of your consumption is covered by solar. Typical factors:

  1. System size vs household demand.
  2. Installation cost (equipment, labor, permits).
  3. Financing: loans reduce short-term net savings but spread cost over time.
  4. Net metering or feed-in tariffs (if available) can change economics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data shared?

No. Calculations are run locally in your browser. Saved CSV files remain on your device unless you choose to share them.

Can I use results to apply for loans or incentives?

The tool provides estimates useful for decision-making, but for financing or permitting you should obtain formal quotes and system proposals from qualified installers.
