Privacy Policy

Effective date:

This website provides tools and informational content about solar savings. We respect your privacy. The site is primarily static and runs tools in your browser; these tools are not guaranteed to work offline and some features may require network access. By default we do not collect personal information or transmit your calculator data to any server unless you explicitly choose to share or upload it.

Data and tools

The Solar Savings Tool performs calculations in your browser and can save results as a CSV file on your device if you choose. That file remains on your device unless you manually share or upload it. We do not upload or store your CSV files on this site unless you explicitly use a sharing/upload feature.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display third-party advertising, such as Google AdSense. Third-party ad networks may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads tailored to your interests and to measure ad performance. Those providers collect information about your visits to this site and other sites, and they may use that information to show you relevant ads.

If you place Google AdSense on this site, include your publisher ID in the AdSense loader script in each page's <head> and add an ads.txt file at the site root. Example ads.txt entry (replace pub-REPLACE_ME with your publisher ID):

google.com, pub-REPLACE_ME, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0

We recommend reading the privacy policies linked by your ad provider (for Google: Google Ads & privacy) for details about data collection and opt-out choices. To opt out of personalized advertising from Google, visit Google Ad Settings.

Links to external resources are provided for convenience and do not imply endorsement. We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices.
