About Solar Savings

This project provides simple, privacy-first tools to help households and small businesses understand the financial impact of switching to solar.

Our goals

  • Give clear, practical calculators to estimate savings and payback.
  • Keep tools offline-friendly and private by running calculations in your browser.
  • Expand to several utilities tools: upcoming Appliances Electricity Calculator, system sizing helper, and comparison charts.

Planned features

Next: an Appliances Electricity Calculator (estimate kWh per appliance and monthly cost). Future updates will add guides for system selection and installer comparison.
