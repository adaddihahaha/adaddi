About Solar Savings

Solar Savings is a focused collection of calculators and utilities for electricity and solar planning. The site groups tools that help estimate bills, model payback, and size small rooftop systems.


What we do

We provide simple, auditable calculators that run in your browser so you can model real scenarios quickly. The core features include monthly bill comparison, estimated 'no-solar' projections, and payback analysis that factors in loan payments and interest.


Our goals

  • Provide clear, practical calculators to estimate savings and payback.
  • Run interactive tools in the browser (note: tools are not guaranteed to run offline and some features may require network access).
  • Expand the toolbox with utilities such as an Appliances Electricity Calculator, a system sizing helper, and comparison charts.

Planned features

Next: an Appliances Electricity Calculator (estimate kWh per appliance and monthly cost). Future updates will add guides for system selection and installer comparison.


Methodology

Calculations use straightforward arithmetic (kWh × price/kWh). We present totals, averages, and simple payback estimates. Results are for planning only and should not replace professional quotes.


FAQ

  • Is my data sent to a server? No — calculations happen in your browser. CSV files are saved locally unless you choose to upload or share them.
  • Can I trust the numbers? The site uses basic formulas. For precise system design, consult a licensed installer.
  • Do you show ads? The site includes ad placeholders. Ads do not affect calculations and are controlled by your publisher settings.
