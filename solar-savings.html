Formulas used:
Before Solar = kWh × ₱/kWh (old bill before solar)
After Solar = kWh × ₱/kWh (actual bill with solar)
Est. Without Solar = No Solar kWh × ₱/kWh — projected bill kung walang solar sa bagong presyo at consumption
Savings = Est. Without Solar − After Solar
% Saved = (Savings ÷ Est. Without Solar) × 100
Net Savings = Savings − Monthly Loan Payment
Total Mortgage = Monthly Loan × Total Months
